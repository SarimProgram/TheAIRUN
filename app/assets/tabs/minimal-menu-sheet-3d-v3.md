# New 3D menu set

Newly generated using the built-in image tool. Satin coral and teal, shallow depth and simple silhouettes.
Applied to Home, Plans, Log, Run, Partner. Original generation kept separately from previous versions.
Export command from repository root: `python app/scripts/export-menu-icons.py`.

## Prompt

Create a brand NEW premium 3D icon asset sheet for a couples fitness mobile app. Exactly FIVE separate icons in ONE horizontal row on a genuinely transparent background, evenly spaced in five equal cells, generous margins and gaps. Order left to right: HOME, PLANS, LOG, RUN, PARTNER. Art direction: precise industrial-design miniature objects made from satin enamel with clean flat front faces, short solid extrusion, tight small edge bevels, subtle physical depth visible along right and lower edges. Crisp architectural geometry, sophisticated restrained rendering, consistent almost-frontal camera and soft upper-left lighting. These must look like premium small dimensional product symbols, NOT puffy clay, not plush, not toys, not inflated balloons. Only coral #FF6B6B and deep teal #087F83, with tonal variations from lighting. No cream or white materials. Home: a compact teal house with a thin coral pitched roof, crisp doorway cutout, no chimney or windows. Plans: a teal rectangular calendar slab with a coral narrow top bar and a single coral check symbol raised slightly on its face; two small teal binding tabs. Log: compact coral camera body with a clean teal cylindrical lens and dark teal lens glass, no extra button details. Run: sleek low-profile teal running shoe with a thin coral outsole, clean sporty silhouette and only two subtle lace marks. Partner: two simple overlapping upright dimensional person bust symbols with round heads and clean shoulder arcs, teal on left and coral on right, faceless, no heart, no hair. Match icon visual mass and height, simplify until recognizable at 30 px. Avoid excessive roundness; use controlled corner radii and clean large faces. No black stroke, no ground plane, no external cast shadows, no glow, no gradient backgrounds, no text or labels, no watermark, no enclosing tiles. Icons fully contained in their cells, no cropping. Landscape transparent PNG icon sheet.
