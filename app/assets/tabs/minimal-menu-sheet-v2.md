## Prompt

Create one cohesive icon asset sheet for the existing couples fitness app shown in the reference images. Reference 1 is the app's rounded 3D coral/teal/cream fitness object style; reference 2 is its friendly character aesthetic, for context only. Match the rounded softly shaded 3D object treatment of reference 1, simplified aggressively for tiny bottom-navigation icons. Exactly FIVE isolated icons arranged in ONE evenly spaced horizontal row, all equal visual size, generous clear margins, no cropping, transparent background. Left to right: HOME: a minimal warm cream house with chunky coral roof and simple teal doorway; PLANS: a chunky coral desk calendar with warm cream face, two cream binding loops and one large teal check mark, no little grid or numbers; LOG: a small rounded coral camera with one prominent deep teal lens and warm cream inner lens; RUN: a single simple teal running shoe, coral heel detail and cream sole, only two broad lace marks; PARTNER: two simple rounded shoulder-and-head bust figurines side by side, one teal and one coral, warm cream heads, one small coral heart floating above their center. Partner icon has no facial features, no hair, no realistic people. Each icon composed of only a few large clean forms, rounded beveled edges, matte satin material, restrained soft studio shading, slight consistent three-quarter view, front readable silhouette. Palette coral #FF6B6B, deep teal #087F83, warm cream #F3E5CD. Consistent lighting upper left. This is minimal 3D iconography, beautifully sculpted and understated, readable at 28-32 px. No black outlines, no text, no labels, no decorative base tiles or circles, no contact shadow outside silhouette, no background, no gradient backdrop, no props, no duplicate states, no realistic textures, no watermark. Preserve actual transparent alpha.
