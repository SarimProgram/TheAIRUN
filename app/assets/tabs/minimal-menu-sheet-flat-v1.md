# Flat coral and teal menu icons

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Applied in app/(tabs)/_layout.tsx.
Columns: Home, Plans, Log, Run, Partner. Transparent PNG.
Run `python app/scripts/export-menu-icons.py` from the repository root to export icons.

## Prompt

Create a single clean production icon sheet for a couples fitness mobile app: exactly FIVE minimalist FLAT 2D icons in ONE evenly spaced horizontal row, each icon centered in its own equal-width invisible cell with ample transparent gaps. Left to right: Home, Plans, Log, Run, Partner. Home: solid deep teal house silhouette with coral roof and transparent open doorway. Plans: solid deep teal calendar with a coral top binding bar and one large transparent check mark in its body. Log: solid deep teal camera silhouette with a coral circular lens and small transparent lens center. Run: simple solid deep teal running shoe side silhouette, coral sole and two short transparent lace cuts. Partner: two overlapping simple bust silhouettes, teal left person, coral right person, with circular heads and rounded shoulders, no heart and no faces. Strictly two flat solid ink colors only: deep teal #087F83 and coral #FF6B6B, plus real transparency. Crisp geometric shapes, restrained rounded corners, generous negative space, perfectly uniform optical size, designed to be clear at 28 pixels. Professional minimal mobile navigation symbols. Absolutely NO 3D, no clay, no bevel, no lighting, no shading, no texture, no gradients, no shadows, no perspective, no extra colors, no black outlines, no text, no labels, no background tiles, no decorative props, no watermark. Transparent background with actual alpha. Landscape sheet, all five icons fully visible with substantial outer margins.
